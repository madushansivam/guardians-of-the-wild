Sri Lanka Focus

A hotspot on
the edge.

Sri Lanka is among the world's top 36 biodiversity hotspots. Over 26% of its mammal species and 33% of flowering plants exist nowhere else on Earth. When they vanish here, they vanish forever.

The island faces a perfect storm — deforestation, rapid urbanisation, human-wildlife conflict, and an illegal exotic pet trade that targets the rarest of the rare.
